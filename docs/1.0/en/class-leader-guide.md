# Class leader guide (Class Hub)

The **Class Hub** shows your class: students, teams (members / capacity), students without a team and upcoming deadlines.
* With the **announce** permission you can post class announcements (and remove your own).
* With the **report** permission you can send an issue to your teachers and follow its status and reply.
* Existing permissions (groups, chats, accounts) work in the admin panel as before.
You cannot assess teams, see passwords, change settings or open other classes.
