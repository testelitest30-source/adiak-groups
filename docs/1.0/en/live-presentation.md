# Live Presentation guide

Open the presentation and press **Open live**. On the classroom display use **Projector view** (full screen, app chrome hidden).

**Stage**: current team and speaker, large timer (remaining time; orange in the last minute, red when over), full order with recorded durations, up next, and the team's approved final (PDF, arrow keys / buttons, full screen).

**Operator console** (teacher): Start live session (freezes teams and speaking orders) · Pause / Resume · Complete team (stops and records the time) · Next team · Previous team (continues its recorded time) · Skip team · Next / previous speaker · Time correction (± minutes, reason required) · End session · Private notes · Assessment.

The timer lives on the server: refreshing, reconnecting, switching devices or putting the phone in the background never loses time. Every control is protected against double clicks (versioned) and audited.
