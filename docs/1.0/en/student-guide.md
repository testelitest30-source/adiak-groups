# Student guide

* **Presentations** — your team's workspace for each presentation:
  * *Overview*: deadline, your team's position after the draw, preparation ring, announcements.
  * *Speaking order*: pick a free slot (you can change it until the team submits). When everyone has a slot, anyone can submit; the host or teacher approves.
  * *Files*: upload drafts (PDF, PPTX, DOCX, images, video… up to 25 MB). Choose **Submit as final**; the teacher approves it or returns it with a note. An approved final is locked. **Upload the final as PDF** so it opens on the live screen.
  * *Tasks*: create, assign, tick off.  *Team chat*: a separate chat for each presentation.  *Feedback*: appears after the teacher publishes it.
* **Classwork** — class announcements and assignments; submit a file and/or a note; see the teacher's feedback.
* **Notifications** — kept on the server, synced across devices; tap one to open the right screen.
